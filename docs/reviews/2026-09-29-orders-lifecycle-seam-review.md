# Orders Lifecycle: verification against the new BSS seams

Date: 2026-09-29. Resumes Claude session `82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821`.

**Verdict: the proposed migration is directionally correct, but it does not yet specify compatible end-to-end contracts.** The checked Workflow ordering and compensation contracts substantially agree with Lifecycle. The commercial seams still contain incompatible assumptions, missing APIs, and proposals awaiting counterpart adoption. In particular, a Pricing renewal pin is not an immutable order-price hold, and copying the Atlas draft SDK would lose required Lifecycle inputs.

This is a static contract/code review, not an implementation or a runtime integration test. No normative design, neighbouring worktree, or source code was changed. The original four Claude review workers produced no completed reports; findings below were checked directly against the sources.

## Findings

### F1 — HIGH / PLAN-ERROR: re-resolving pins does not preserve the submitted price

The plan promises a submit-to-activation hold and a first-period binding with `pinned_from = None` ([A plan](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/seams/common.md:30)). Actual Pricing dispatches **every supplied pin** to `renewal`, sets `pinned_from`, walks eligible `all` successors, and can fall back to a signup when the old binding has ended ([P resolve](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/products-wt/gears/bss/pricing/pricing/src/domain/resolve.rs:269), [renewal](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/products-wt/gears/bss/pricing/pricing/src/domain/resolve.rs:287)). Subscriptions' adopted contract says a signup sends no pins and renewal uses period-start pins ([P SUB-D-29](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/products-wt/gears/bss/subscriptions/docs/DECISIONS.md:272)).

Example: an order selects price A; price B with eligibility `all` starts before activation. `resolve(activation_date, pins=[A])` can return B. Immutability of A's money does not prevent that selection change. `keep_for_bound` is not a universal no-reprice switch.

**Required correction — Pricing + Subscriptions + Orders:** specify an explicit initial-binding/offer-acceptance contract, distinct from renewal. Define hold expiry, explicit price closure, temporary-price expiry, descriptor provenance, and first-period slicing. Decide whether activation honours the submitted price or requires re-quotation/approval. Do not silently implement a hold by feeding pins into ordinary resolve.

### F2 — HIGH / PLAN-ERROR: existing TTLs do not bound that hold

The proposal calls the hold TTL-bounded ([A plan](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/seams/common.md:30); the recovered conversation explicitly relies on existing TTLs). Lifecycle excludes `in_fulfillment` and holds from fulfillment from expiry; unset TTLs also leave states unbounded ([L expiry](/Users/nichmor/orca/workspaces/gears-rust/bss-orders-design-doc/gears/bss/orders-lifecycle/docs/features/07-hold-and-expiry.md:110), [limits](/Users/nichmor/orca/workspaces/gears-rust/bss-orders-design-doc/gears/bss/orders-lifecycle/docs/features/07-hold-and-expiry.md:131)). Wave 1 and future activation waits occur after begin-fulfillment.

**Required correction — Orders + Subscriptions:** define an absolute commercial offer deadline if one is required, including how Workflow and activation enforce it. A state TTL or operational escalation SLA is not that deadline. This invalidates the blanket claim that hold/expiry needs no consideration during migration.

### F3 — HIGH / GAP: the gate needs more than the proposed resolve trait

The plan checks published revision, sale date and SKU sellability ([A gate](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/seams/common.md:20)). Resolve accepts both published and superseded revisions ([P read](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/products-wt/gears/bss/pricing/pricing/src/api/rest/read_contract.rs:412)); its DTO has no `available_from` ([P DTO](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/products-wt/gears/bss/pricing/pricing/src/api/rest/read_contract/dto.rs:13)). `available_from` is nullable in the revision contract ([P revision](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/products-wt/gears/bss/pricing/pricing/src/domain/plan.rs:42)). The Atlas's `current_revision`/`resolve` draft alone does not establish a complete sale-date predicate.

**Required correction — Pricing + Orders:** define current-revision and availability reads, null/date/timezone semantics and a shared prospective-purchase verdict. A successful resolve is not sale eligibility. Keep separate rules for new purchases and activation of an already accepted revision: superseding a revision must not accidentally invalidate an agreed hold. Preserve failed versus unavailable diagnostics. Orders-composed sellability is an explicit ownership change from [L adopt-not-fork](/Users/nichmor/orca/workspaces/gears-rust/bss-orders-design-doc/gears/bss/orders-lifecycle/docs/UPSTREAM_REQS.md:131), not a transparent adapter.

### F4 — HIGH / PLAN-ERROR: item selection and pin encoding need an explicit adapter

The proposal checks paid items and carries a dimension per item ([A plan](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/seams/common.md:20)). PriceBook has paid, optional and included items; an unpriced included item has no chains ([P item](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/products-wt/gears/bss/pricing/pricing/src/domain/resolve.rs:62)). Therefore the gate/evaluator must include chosen optional items, preserve included allowances and quantities, and validate the selected chain rather than reject every uncovered cell in the returned matrix.

The order's **selected dimension** is also not always Pricing's `Pin.dim_value`. Pricing permits that field only when pinning a default-chain price for a particular value ([P Pin](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/products-wt/gears/bss/pricing/pricing/src/domain/resolve.rs:32), [validation](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nichmor-orca-workspaces-gears-rust-bss-orders-design-doc/82d80cd0-348e-4ae1-ae95-aee2ea5f5821/scratchpad/products-wt/gears/bss/pricing/pricing/src/domain/resolve.rs:196)). Passing `{price_id: own_chain_price, dim_value: selected_value}` is `PIN_FOREIGN`. Preserve `dim_used` or otherwise recover the price's own chain and encode the consumer pin correctly.

**Required correction — Orders + Pricing + Rating:** define per-item selection, quantity and dimension semantics, optional-item membership validation, and exact round-trip pin conversion. Specify revision **per line** plus an assessment date; replacing one catalog version per basket with one revision per entire basket would exclude orders for different plans. The market check also needs an explicit mapping: an arbitrary PriceBook dimension is not automatically the payer's region ([L market rule](/Users/nichmor/orca/workspaces/gears-rust/bss-orders-design-doc/gears/bss/orders-lifecycle/docs/features/03-gate-and-pin.md:156)).

## Corrections to the earlier migration plan

1. Treat PriceBook adoption by Rating/Subscriptions as **decided, adapter deferred**, not as an unmade adoption decision. Legacy detailed text is migration debt, not the active target when superseded by T-D-37/SUB-D-29.
2. Add a jointly agreed initial-binding/offer contract before claiming submit-to-activation price stability. Keep renewal and commercial expiry separate.
3. Agree overlap policy, count/read shape and atomic activation enforcement together. Keep `plan_id` proposed until both sides adopt the policy.
4. Make sellability ownership explicit; add availability/current-revision access, selected optional items, pin encoding, date and market semantics. A successful resolve is insufficient.
5. Specify the complete Rating result and preserve fail-closed submit until an alternative approval/payment flow is designed.
6. Derive the Workflow SDK from Lifecycle's full contracts, correcting the Atlas's missing authority and line mapping. Update Workflow's PriceBook reads/re-check/provisioning inputs in the same design change.
7. Carry topology, SKU protection and expanded payload budgets into readiness criteria. Preserve existing start, compensation, correlation and idempotency asks.

## Verification scenarios required before calling the seams integrated

- Submit at A, publish an `all` successor B before activation, and prove the chosen initial-binding policy; repeat across a temporary/explicit end and a fulfillment hold exceeding state TTLs.
- Resolve an own-value chain versus a default fallback, including a chosen optional item, an unpriced included allowance, and an uncovered selected chain. Round-trip each pin without `PIN_FOREIGN`.
- Resolve two different plan revisions in one order under the seller scope with a different buyer/payer; prove denied and unavailable outcomes remain distinct internally.
- Reconcile occupancy with wave-1 drafts, limit greater than one, alternate plans for one family, and concurrent activation; prove enforcement at Subscriptions' commit.
- Return whole-order TCV with monthly/annual/open-ended terms, one-time charges and minimum fees; prove usage exclusion and no silent missing/zero substitution.
- Reflect all four approval verdicts with authority; complete two reordered lines with explicit mappings; replay after timeout, hold and amendment.
- Refuse unknown topology and exercise SKU retirement during the gap between checks and activation.
- Serialize the largest allowed event and batch the largest allowed purchase without violating pin, payload or latency limits.

These scenarios are proposed acceptance checks, not tests executed during this review. Payments, payer-profile/Contracts APIs and platform event delivery retain the existing open requirements in `UPSTREAM_REQS.md`; their full implementations were outside this focused PriceBook/seam review.
